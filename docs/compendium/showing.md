# What the compendium shows

How the book becomes what lists and pages show under Loose and Strict, what is kept back until found out, and tallies of what was seen.

Code: `src/Model/Explorer.cs`, `src/Model/CompendiumBook.cs`, `src/Model/CompendiumWords.cs`, `src/UI/ScryPanel.cs`, `src/Catalog/Facts/Facts.cs`, `src/Model/Search.cs`, `src/Model/CompendiumCatchUp.cs`, `src/Model/EntryKeys.cs`.

See also: [learning](learning.md), [sources](sources.md), [pages](../details/pages.md); all notes in [the index](../index.md).

## What shows

An entry shows as the book holds it, mods are always known, and a skill once the character has learned it (see the skills above). What a known entry owns is known with it, but not what an entry only seen owns, and under *Strict* a dungeon known brings along only the kinds of room the character has stood in, read from its dungeon maps of every world, as all else it met counts in every world (`WalkedRooms`, `DungeonMaps.RoomsWalked`, `CompendiumView.RoomsWalked`), the rest named a room not yet walked; so a creature seen from afar opens none of its attacks: what it plays, what it leaves behind, and the status effects, effects, sounds and projectiles it links to as its own. Links are followed forward only (`Link.Back`), so knowing Poison does not make known all that gives it. A boss stone does not own its guardian power, which it gives only for the boss's trophy (`Giver.IsGuardianPower`): knowing the stone tells of the power, and the power is known once chosen at it, or by a floor reaching its own tier. Lists and search hold only what is seen or known; a tab counts what was found of all there is for the kinds `CompendiumView.Counted` names, and a tab with nothing found is hidden. They hold only the world a player meets, its creatures, items, pieces, resources, spawners, status effects, biomes, places and raids, not the effects, sounds, projectiles, other parts and mods the game is built of, which come back with the compendium off or while peeking (`Kinds.ForPlayers`, `Search.Run`).

What exists only through a thing the character has not met is left out whole rather than named ???, as the topic would tell the thing is there: a creature's *Riding* topic and its saddled look on the stage until its saddle is met, its breeding until its young is, its growing up until what it grows into is (`Facts.PartHinges`, `PrefabLooks.HingeOf`, `Explorer.HidesHinged`); seen counts as met. A creature's gear choices under its look name an item never met ???, and a look of a set of gear holding one is not offered, as it would show the item and its name tell its attack (`ScryPanel.LoadoutRows`, `Explorer.LooksShown`). A boss's *After it falls* rows are left out whole while nothing they name was met, as their titles alone tell what its fall opens (`Facts.Row.UnmetLeftOut`).

*Learned* and its notice tell what was met under *Loose*, and under *Strict* also what was found out and what was seen go together in play, as news of the thing acted on by how the two go together, a drop seen fall, what a thing gave, ate or held, where it is found, what broke it (`CompendiumView.NewsOf`, `CompendiumBook.SawWith`, `Tie`), once each, none while caught up; they count the same, and while a search is typed the *Learned* tab counts what it finds among what was learned, as each kind's tab does, so it goes when that is nothing (`Explorer.LearnedCount`). A row shows no prefab name then, nor does a page's header, where it shows on the name's hover, and entries of one kind sharing a name say which of them each is, by the words their prefabs do not share or else as "2 of 3" (`Entry.Alike`, told by `Search.OrderNames`). A row's tag too wide for it keeps its last part, the one telling rows alike apart ("Crypt2" of "Burial Chambers dungeon · Crypt2"), and is cut with an ellipsis only after that, left out for the hover where the cut would keep less than three fifths of it (`TextFit.Tag`); a name too long for the row is cut with an ellipsis, and with the compendium on a row shows only which of several alike it is, never a place's tag, which holds its prefab. A table too wide for its room shrinks what each column wants beyond its heading first, so the headings stay whole (`TableFit`). A dungeon room is named by its prefab without its dungeon's tag, the words the game runs together apart ("sloperoom" as "slope room") and without the Frost Caves' leading "new" where no room of that name without it is there (`LocationWords.RoomName`).

Biomes are named as the game names them in its language, in the tiers and the search's suggestions as on their pages (`BiomeWords.Shown`). A seen entry stands on the turntable with its page closed, telling every way to come to know it (`CompendiumWords.HowToKnow`), and under *Strict* that some of its page shows only once found out; a known one shows its page. The names a known page's text keeps back are those of the world a player meets only (`Kinds.ForPlayers`): an effect, a sound or a projectile is never met, and its name ("Fire") is often a plain word of the text ("fires"). Lines of a page one deed shows say what shows them once, the next ones on hover. A trader's page is laid out by topic ("What it sells"), so its section ends with what the compendium left out as every page's does, and a key a trader sets for an item brought to it (`Trader.m_useItems`, Hildir's chests) is told by that item. The compendium's words keep one verb for each thing: a page is come to know, a number held back is found out, and *Learned* names both; they say "you" for what the player does and "this character" only where characters are told apart. A known page under either rules names only what the character has met, seen or come to know (`Explorer.NamesOnlyMet`, `Explorer.LeavesOut`): the rest is left out as *Strict* leaves out what was not seen together, below, each section ending in a faint line counting it, "2 more not yet met" (`CompendiumWords.NotYetMet`, `Explorer.LeftOutLine`); names never met read ??? in its text and row titles; and search finds a known entry through what it names only by what was met (`Search.Guarded`).

What the floor shows counts as met, and *Loose* keeps the numbers of a page whole. A place's example stands no creature the character has not met (`Explorer.NotMet`, `Stage.CreatureLeftOff`), and a room's card counts those left out the same way. Where the game gives a thing no name, or names an attack in its own shorthand, a page names it by its prefab's words in sentence case, without numbers or the tags that tell how it is built (`Naming.Readable`: "rock4_heath_frac" as "Rock heath", "slap" as "Slap"; an attack known only by such a code name that fires something goes by what it fires, "Troll throw" for the troll's "fireballattack" (`CombatWords.FiredName`)), its prefab on hover. An item's page tells where it comes from in a row for each way that gives an amount or nothing, dropped, picked, grown or out of a drop table, each giver a chip, givers going by one name but for their biome or the number a mod tells its variants apart by ("Rock (4)", "Marker (01)") one chip saying how many with the range of what they give, as "1–3 Rock ×9", each giver's own name, line and prefab on hover (`SourceGroups`, `Naming.WithoutBiome`, `Naming.WithoutVariant`, `ScryPanel.SplitWhere`); a way with a pace, a price or a birth keeps its line, and under *Strict* each giver keeps a chip of its own, its count the character's after its name (`Explorer.StrictSourceAmount`), what shows a count held back faint under the rows.

The game's commands that spawn or give a thing are offered only with the compendium off or while peeking (`Explorer.ShowsCommands`). *Learned* lists everything the character learned, newest first (`CompendiumBook.Lately`), under a heading for each game day, the newest day and the one before open and the rest folded, what has no day (caught up from the game's records, or kept before days were) last under *Earlier* (`ScryPanel.List.cs`, `JournalWords.DayHeading`, `FoldedAtFirst`): what came to be known, saying how ("killed", "picked"), what was only seen, faint, and what was found out of something held back under *Strict*, which brings its entry back to the top saying what, as "its health found out" (`CompendiumBook.NewsOf`, `JournalWords.LearnedNote`, kept with the book; found out as a character loads is no news). A row's page opens at what changed: the line showing what was found out, by the proof it was found by, else the *You* box's line telling how it came to be known or was first seen, else the topic that holds it, In the game unfolded, the details scrolled to it and the line lit a moment in the look of the list's row picked, fading (`NewsArrival`, `ScryPanel.Arrival.cs`). What a character's first load brings from the game's records is news as well, without a record of when or where it came to be known, as the records do not say (`CompendiumCatchUp.Fill`, `firstLoad`). What a reading of the explored map brings, the first too, is news noted "seen on your explored map" (`EntryKeys.OnMap`, `CompendiumBook.NoteNews`), and the notice under the header counts it rather than naming it, "your explored map showed 240 places", as it may be hundreds at once. The notice tells the rest in words of what happened, by day: "Day 14: came to know Troll and Greydwarf Brute, saw 2 more, and found out how fire lands on Neck", over several days "Since day 12", naming while there are few and counting what the game's records told (`JournalWords.News`).

Each session reads a world's map once again (`CompendiumMap.Update`), so ground explored since counts too. The eye beside the compendium's button peeks at the page shown alone (`Explorer.PeekPage`, `Explorer.PeekedPage`): it shows whole and its links lead anywhere until another entry is gone to, by the list, a link, a step back or a map's mark, a link to a thing the character has not met opening that page peeked at in turn; with no page shown it peeks at every page. The notice's *Every page* peeks at every page until the panel closes (`Explorer.Peeking`, `Explorer.PeekingEvery`), a whole name in quotes then finding what the character has not met. Either way the list, its counts and the search stay as the character knows them (`Explorer.CompendiumSet` for the list, `Explorer.CompendiumOn` for the page), a page reached by a link staying while the list is asked again; a dungeon map peeks at every room not walked. A page opens with a box titled *You*: what the game counted of the thing for the character, how often it killed, built, ate or picked it and how many of it it made and picked up (`CharacterRecords.Counts`, from the profile's own statistics), and with the compendium on how it came to know it, else what known brought it along, else the floor's tier that shows it ("shown as known up to the Black Forest tier"), else known before Scry kept track of this character (`CompendiumWords.CameLine`). Under *Strict* it ends with what the page still keeps back, a row for each thing to do and, faint, what it shows: kill one, tame one, see one born (a creature's breeding), grow up or grow back, see a crop grow (its time to grow, `Plant.Grow`) or a smelter, kiln, cooking station or fermenter finish one (its times and fuel, `Deed.Finished`), hit it with the types whose landing is held back, let the attacks not yet taken hit you, hit its weak spots, see it drop or break one (`CompendiumWords.StillToFindOut`, gathered from the page's facts). A held-back fact reads ???, what shows it on its hover, and is said nowhere else on the page but the *You* box's list of what is still to find out and the title row's count: one place for each thing to do. A faint line at a section's end is left only for names it leaves out, as those are gone from the page. The title row tells it at a glance, before copy and star: a chip counting how much of what the page keeps back was found out, "7 of 12 found out" with a small bar, each deed's number once, each weak spot, damage type, attack's damage and list of counts (`PageFinds`, `ScryPanel.FindsChip`), and each name the page could leave out, shown or left out, as the last whole draw of its details counted them (`ScryPanel.StrictShown`, `StrictLeft`), its hover the still-to-find-out lines and how many names are left out, and a click going to the lines in the *You* box, lit; lit itself with a tick once all of it was found out and no section naming things was folded, a folded one going uncounted; faint, with the stairs, where *Known up to* or a source of the world shows the page whole instead ("Whole by Known up to", `Explorer.ShownWhole`), its hover saying how to see what is left.  Under *Strict* a creature's row of what was seen dropping in all your play, every character's, and the matching lines on items' pages give way to the character's own counts (`Facts.Row.AllPlay`, `Source.AllPlay`).

Under *Strict*, only a known entry brings its parts along, and a known page above the floor keeps of how it connects only what the character saw go together with it, or what is a part of it (`CompendiumView.Related`, `Explorer.LeavesOut`, `ScryPanel.ShownStrictly`). A place stood in goes together with the biome it stands in (`CompendiumCatchUp.StoodIn`); a station with each upgrade the game counts for it, as it is used, as an upgrade is built in its reach and as it is built near its upgrades (`StationExtension.FindExtensions`, `CraftingStation.FindClosestStationInRange`, `CompendiumCatchUp.Upgrades`). A rune stone read keeps the text it gave as read in the book, whatever the game kept of it, as the game keeps a text only where it has a label (`CompendiumBook.ReadText`); its hook acts on every read but a hold, as `RuneStone.Interact` answers false to every one. Both keep a text by the key the stone gives it by, while a location's page tells its texts in words, so each told text keeps its key and is asked after by it (`RuneText.Key`, `RuneWords.ReadAs`), in whatever language it is shown. The self-test feeds each of these ways through the keeper's own entry points into a book of its own and asks the strict rules what then goes together, and reads the code of every game method a hook of Scry's acts on the answer of, failing where it can only answer false (`IlShape.OnlyFalse`), as `RuneStone.Interact` and `MapTable.OnRead` do. The rest is left out: a fact naming something else, a table's row, a place it is found in; a row or table left with nothing draws nothing, its title neither. Each section that left something out ends in a faint line saying how many more there are, worded by what the section is about, such as drops not yet seen fall or places it lives not yet met in (`CompendiumWords.LeftOutOf`, by the topic's id in the page's plan), never what they are; every place the panel trims counts what it drops, and each topic, the effects and where an effect plays start the count afresh (`ScryPanel.StrictLeftLine`).

Such a line, as every line telling what is still to find (a number kept back, weak spots not found, what is not yet counted, how many wait to unlock), is set apart from the page's footnotes, which stay faint: an accent bar and a compass at its start, its words a step brighter (`ScryPanel.HintLine`). A trader's wares waiting for a boss not yet fallen in the world are named by neither rules: under *Loose* each such row keeps its title with how many it holds, rows whose titles read alike once a boss not met is kept back ("Sells once ??? is defeated") told as one wherever they stand (`UnlockRows.Told`), under *Strict* they are counted at the section's end, "N more to unlock as bosses fall" (`UnlockRows`). Pairs are noted in play, a drop seen fall from a creature, a creature met in a biome and a place, a creature's weapon hitting the character, a tool hitting a resource, a container's loot and the place it stands in, a raid with the creatures it brings that were met, an item picked up with the biome and place it lay in, a piece placed with the tool in hand, a creature with what it was seen eat, a trader with its wares, what went into a thing used, an item coming to be within 8 m of a thing used or hit in the last ten minutes that gives it (`ActedOn`; the ore seen going in pairs with the bar that came out), a boss called at its altar with the altar, its offering and its place, a fish with its bait and where it swam, a Vegvisir with the places it marks; and from the records, a recipe with its ingredients and station, a trophy with its creature, a thing picked with what gives it in the biomes entered, a food with its effects, a power chosen with its stone, a piece placed with the one tool that builds it where only one does.

Search finds a known entry by how it connects only through what was seen (`Search.Run`), and a known creature by how a damage type lands on it only once a hit told that, and by what it deals only through the attacks that hit the character (`Entry.Attacks`, `SearchFight.DealtBy`); its things to bring show once what suggests them was found out (`Facts.Ingredient.ShownBy`, `Explorer.KeepsBackAll`). In a page's text and the game's tooltip card on it, the names of things never met read ??? (`NameMask`, `ScryPanel.CardShown`). A mod's page is never trimmed. A known creature's damage grid, and that of anything that breaks (a tree, a log, a rock, a vein, a bone pile; `Facts.Row.HitReveals`), keeps back how it takes each type until a hit of the character's told it, as the game's popup tells it on both alike (`Destructible`, `TreeBase`, `TreeLog`, `MineRock` and `MineRock5` show it as a creature does; a hit too weak for its tool tier only says it is too hard, `HitData.CheckToolTier`, `ResourceHitWatch`): the game's popup on a hit shows one kind of landing, the one most of its damage took, so on the character's hit Scry runs the game's own `HitData.ApplyResistance` on a copy, as the game does on receiving it, and pairs the creature with each type that landed so (`HitTold`, `EntryKeys.Damage`); a hit on a weak spot lands by the weak spot's own modifiers, so it finds that spot instead (`EntryKeys.WeakSpot`). A type kept back reads ??? in its cell, saying on hover what shows it, a tool's cell stays.

Other numbers the game never shows are kept back the same way until the character found them out, each saying what shows it (`Explorer.KeepsBack`, `Facts.Proofs`, `Facts.Row.LineProofs`). An attack's damage and what it puts on you read ??? until that attack hit the character, its weapon paired with the creature (`Explorer.HidesAttack`, `Facts.Row.AttackCells`), each ??? saying on hover what shows it. A creature's health, in its facts and by its stars, shows once the character killed one, from the game's own kill count (`CompendiumCatchUp.Fill`); how long taming and a meal take once the character saw one tamed (seen wild, then tame; whether a creature is tame is kept on it for every player to see, so a taming is seen whichever player's game does it, where `Tameable.Tame` runs only in the game owning the creature; not petting); how long it is pregnant once the character saw one born (`Procreation.Procreate`, its pregnancy ending); how long a young one takes to grow up once the character saw one do it (`Growup.GrowUpdate`, the young one gone); how long a pickable takes to grow back once the character saw one do it (`Pickable.SetPicked` to not picked). Each hook counts how often it fired this session (`PlayCounts`, `CompendiumKeeper.Noted`), and the self-test tells the counts, zeros among them, so ordinary play shows which really ran on this machine. The self-test also feeds made-up events through the real hooks into a book of its own (`CompendiumKeeper.RecordInto`): a hit on a loaded creature and on its weak spot, an armed creature's hit taken, a birth, a regrowth, and a taming near by, one creature told wild, then tame (`CompendiumKeeper.Tamings`), a tree breaking by the character's hit with an item come to be, a death beyond sight that the character had no hand in and one it had, told twice, a map read; nothing in the world changes, and neither the character's book nor the counts do.

It reads a made-up character's troll and troll hide pages under *Strict* as a player sees them, every part held back drawn and its text as said. A deed counts as every event of the world does, below: no one does a birth, a growing up and the like, so the character has to see it, within 30 m, and, a taming aside, it runs only where the game does it on this machine, as for the thing's owner; each is a pair of the thing with a deed's key (`EntryKeys.Did`, `DeedWatch`), a fact it shows reading ???, "shows once you have ..." on its hover. A weak spot no hit found is left out, the *You* box counting those. A creature's drops and what a tree, log, rock or other thing gives as it breaks show what the character saw, not the game's chances: each item how many at a time and in how many of the kills or breaks seen, the row's title how many there were, ??? for one never counted, its hover saying what counts it (`Explorer.StrictAmount`, `Tally`). They keep the game's own form, the count where the game puts its chance, as "5–8 (12 of 12)" (`TallyWords.Chip`). On an item's page each line of where it comes from that holds a chance is told the same way, as "Dropped by Troll, 5–8 (12 of 12 kills)" or "Felled from Beech, 2–3 (14 of 14 times)" (`Explorer.StrictSourceLine`, `SourceWords.CountedBy`); what a chest held is counted the first time the character opens each chest the world fills (`InventoryGui.Show`, `CompendiumKeeper.Opened`, the chest's id kept in the book), as "Found in Treasure chest, 30–50 (2 of 5 opened)"; what a bush gives besides is counted at picking (`Pickable.RPC_Pick` in `YieldWatch`); a mod's own part is counted nowhere and reads ???, saying so on hover; a producer's line of what it makes reads ??? until collected from one (`SourceWords.ShownBy`); ways with no chance in them stay as the game tells them.

More numbers wait for a deed of their own: a tree's, log's or rock's health until one broke (a break counted); what stars change past health, by the stars table and the drops with stars, until a starred creature was seen to fall (its level with its death, `CharacterDrop.OnDeath` or `Ragdoll.Setup`); a spawner's pool, star chance and pace until it was seen to spawn within sight (`SpawnArea.SpawnOne`); a beehive's or sap collector's pace until the character collected from one holding anything (`Beehive.Interact`, `SapCollector.Interact`, its level as the game keeps it). A table every line of which waits so, as a creature's stars table before one was killed, is its title and ???, what shows it on hover (`Explorer.KeepsBackWhole`). A biome's weathers show how often each was seen come there in place of the game's share, each change of weather in the biome stood in counted once (`EnvMan.GetCurrentEnvironment`, `GetCurrentBiome`, `CompendiumBook.Weathered`), ??? for one never seen, its hover saying so. The counts are the character's own, kept in the book (`CompendiumBook.Dropped`, `Yielded`): a creature's loot as `DropWatch` sees it come out, and every item that comes to be while a thing breaks or loses a piece (`TreeBase.RPC_Damage`, `TreeLog.Destroy`, `MineRock.RPC_Hit`, `MineRock5.DamageArea`, `DropOnDestroyed.OnDestroyed`; `YieldWatch`), a break counted where it gave anything. A creature's or resource's line of where it spawns or grows shows only where it was met in one of the biomes it names (`Source.MetIn`, `Explorer.LeavesOutAll`).

Every death with its loot, break or pick with what it gave, birth, growing up, regrowth, crop grown, spawn, station's output and creature eating that this game runs is made into one event as it runs (`WorldEvents`, `WorldEvent`): what happened, to what, where, what came of it, the creature's level, and who did it, each player by their game's session, as the game addresses a player's game. Who did it is what the game itself knows: the players a creature is flagged as hit by, by name, as the game flags them on each hit (`Character.ApplyDamage`) and tells each of them the kill (`Character.OnDeath`); the player whose hit broke a thing, or destroyed one that drops what it holds as it goes (`Destructible.Destroy`, `WearNTear.Destroy`); the player whose game asked for a pick (`Pickable.RPC_Pick`); no one for the rest. One rule records an event for the character (`EventRule`): it did it, however far away it happened, or it saw it, within 30 m of where it happened as the event is told and with a line from the eye to a person's height over it that meets nothing solid, or stops within a step and a half of it on what the deed was done to, as a place or a thing is seen (`CompendiumSight.SeesDeed`, `Sighting.DeedInSight`); else nothing of it is recorded. An event goes by the game that ran it and its number there, so one told twice counts once (`EventLedger`, forgotten with the world). What the character does in its own game, as collecting from a producer, opening a chest or a hit telling how damage lands, is recorded as it does it.

With other players near, one player's game runs each thing around them, the first to come near it until they move away (`ZDOMan.ReleaseNearbyZDOS`), and only that game sees it fall, break, be picked, spawn, be born, grow up or eat; so the events cross between games (`EventLink`, above).
