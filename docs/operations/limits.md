# Limits

What Scry knowingly does not do or cannot know.

See also: [network](../network.md), [server](../server.md); all notes in [the index](../index.md).

## Limits

A mod prefab whose look is built by its own scripts may preview bare. Figures at the character's skill leave out what is worn and the effects on it besides those adding levels, and a mod changing the game's skill rules is noted rather than followed. A dungeon location's own copy leaves out its interior, which the game builds with the zone, and its rooms stand on the stage only as an example laid out by Scry, and a location's pieces show whole where the game may wear them at random when it builds the zone. The stage is lit by its own lights, so materials that depend on the world's lighting can look different there. A few things stand in for what the game does with a target and are approximations: where a swing's hit lands, an attack with no strike marked in its clips, steps heard where a foot comes down, one projectile thrown for a burst, and clips paired only by their names. Under the compendium's *Strict* rules, a tie no watcher sees stays left out of a known page: a creature tamed far from the character, one fed far from it or on the client of another player who owns it, and whatever was done before Scry watched beyond what the game's records keep. What another player's game runs is counted only for the character's own kills, breaks and picks, and then only as told above.
